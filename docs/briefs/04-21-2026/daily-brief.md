### pi-hole / pi-hole — A black hole for Internet advertisements

Pi-hole is a network-level ad blocker that acts as a DNS sinkhole, blocking Internet advertisements. It can be installed on a Raspberry Pi or other devices, and works by filtering out requests to known ad servers. By blocking ads at the network level, Pi-hole can improve browsing speed, reduce data usage, and enhance overall online experience. With over 1.5 million installations, Pi-hole has become a popular solution for those looking to minimize online ads. Its open-source nature and ease of use have contributed to its widespread adoption.
